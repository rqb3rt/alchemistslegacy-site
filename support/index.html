Support

Something not working, or a question about the game? Write to support@alchemistslegacy.com.

It helps to include your phone model, the level you were on and what happened. A screenshot is welcome.

Common questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your phone. Deleting the game deletes your progress, and we have no copy to restore it from.

Can every level be solved?

Yes. Every level is checked to be winnable before it goes in the game. Some are meant to be hard, and a level can still be lost by what you take and when.

How do I stop sharing gameplay statistics?

Open Settings in the game's menu and turn off Analytics. You can change your answer whenever you like.

How do I ask about my data?

Our Privacy Policy explains what the game uses and your rights. For a request, write to the address above.

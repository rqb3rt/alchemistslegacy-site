Welcome to the workshop

Nana Wren has kept these shelves for a lifetime, and now the cauldron is yours. Pick the ingredients you need, place them in your tray and watch the potion brew the moment a recipe is complete. But the tray only holds five, and the shelves are deeper than they look: what you take now decides what you can reach later.

  • No timer. Think as long as you like.
  • Brew to soft rain, a crackling fireplace, ocean waves, a birdsong forest or the music.
  • 1,000 levels on a gently rising curve, every one of them solvable.
  • Over 200 ingredients and 220 recipes to brew.
